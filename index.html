Birds belong outside. Not on your balcony.

Pigeons and other birds nesting on balconies, ledges, shopfronts and rooftops create droppings, noise, damage and hygiene concerns. Existing fixes — nets, spikes, repellents — can be unsightly, hard to maintain, or harmful to the animals.

Koupoo proposes a different approach: short, controlled bursts of sound, tuned to be an unwelcome signal for birds, delivered by a compact wall-mounted unit. The goal is simple — make the space uncomfortable to settle in, without harming anyone.

How it works

The concept: a wall-mounted unit releases short, controlled sound bursts across several frequency bands, then stays quiet. Birds are expected to find the area unpleasant and move on. Tap a band below to preview it.

Burst B + C + D + E (4 × 0.5 s)Quiet interval

Demo timing is sped up. The plan is one burst roughly every minute, with target output of 50–65 dB. Both are R&D targets, to be verified in testing.

1. Detect

Motion sensing wakes the unit when something approaches (planned).

2. Burst

Four 0.5 s bands play in sequence: B, C, D, then E.

3. Rest

Quiet between bursts instead of constant noise.

4. Repeat

Varied patterns aim to stop birds getting used to it. A hypothesis to test.

Safety and testing

Select for details

Koupoo is a concept in development. We do not present the following as established fact until they are properly verified:

  • Deterrence performance and coverage range
  • Animal safety, including effects on non-target animals and pets
  • Human hearing safety
  • Any ultrasonic output claims

All will be subject to laboratory testing, certification and regulatory compliance before any product is sold or any claim is made.
